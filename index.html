Focused — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Focused automatically switches your phone to vibrate when you walk through a door fitted with Focused door devices, or when you arrive at a place or reach a time you have saved, and restores your ringer when you leave. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your information.

Focused has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. There is no server that belongs to us, and we do not receive, store, or have access to any of your data.

Information the app uses

InformationWhy
Approximate and precise location To detect when you arrive at and leave a place you have saved, and to set a backup "you have left" point when you walk through a Focused door. This includes use in the background while the app is closed.
Nearby Bluetooth devices To hear Focused door devices. The app only reacts to devices carrying Focused's own identifier and ignores every other Bluetooth device. Android treats Bluetooth scanning as a form of location.
Addresses you type into search To look up a place when you create a rule.

Location in the background

Focused uses location data to enable automatic vibrate at Focused doors and automatic vibrate at your saved places, even when the app is closed or not in use. The app shows you this explanation and asks you to agree before it requests any location permission. If you decline, door detection and saved places stay off and timed rules still work.

Your location is used on your phone only. It is not sent to us or to anyone else while the app watches for arrivals and departures. That work is done on the device by Android itself.

Focused door devices

The devices mounted at a door only broadcast a short identifier a few times a second. They do not connect to your phone, receive anything from it, or record who walks past. The organisation that installed them learns nothing about you from them.

Where information is sent

Almost all of the app works entirely on your device. Information leaves your phone only in these two cases, both when you are adding a place to a rule:

Sent toWhat is sentWhy
Google Maps Platform Text you type into address search, and your location while a map is displayed To suggest addresses and to draw the map.
Google (Android system) Coordinates of a point you tap on the map To convert that point into a street address, using Android's built-in service.

Information kept on your device only

  • The rules you create, including their names, addresses and coordinates
  • Whether you are currently inside a Focused place or a saved place, and when a door device was last heard
  • Your settings, such as the rule radius, and whether you agreed to the location explanation
  • A local diagnostic log (focused.log) recording app errors

None of this is transmitted to us or to anyone else. If Android Backup is switched on for your device, Android may include these files in your own Google account backup; that backup is controlled by you and by Google, not by us.

Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionWhy
Location, including in the background The core function: detecting door devices, and arrival at and departure from saved places, while your phone is in your pocket.
Nearby devices (Bluetooth) To hear Focused door devices.
Notifications To show a notice while Focused is holding your ringer, so you always know the app is active and can undo it with one tap.
Do Not Disturb access Only required for rules set to full silent. Vibrate works without it.
Alarms and reminders Only required for rules that switch at a set time, and to keep the app's backup checks on time.
Ignore battery optimisation Android otherwise stops the app after a few days, and it would silently stop working.

Retention and deletion

All app data is stored on your device. Uninstalling Focused removes it. You can also clear it at any time from Android Settings, under Apps → Focused → Storage. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Children

Focused is not directed at children and does not collect information from anyone.
